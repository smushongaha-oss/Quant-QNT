    How the score is built

    Zone (25). Order blocks form from the last opposite candle before a structure break (BOS or CHoCH). Broken swing levels become flip zones (support turned resistance and back). Points for how impulsive the leg was, how fresh the zone is (first touch scores highest), and whether two zones stack.

    Structure (25). H1 bias agrees with the trade (10), entry timeframe trend agrees (7), and the signal candle swept liquidity beyond a swing or beyond the zone before closing back (8).

    Candle (20). Engulfing, morning or evening star, pin bar, harami, double pin and double doji score by strength. A long wick against the trade costs points, which is the "no entry" case in the notebook sheets.

    Momentum (15). SMA 10 above or below SMA 20 with slope, plus a fresh Stochastic RSI cross out of the opposite half.

    Regime (15). ATR compared with its 50 bar average. Normal volatility scores full marks, dead or spiking markets score low.

    Trade plan. Entry at the signal close, stop beyond the zone or wick plus 0.15 ATR, targets at 1R, 2R and 3R. Stops are checked before targets inside the same candle, so results lean pessimistic.

    The scanner. All 13 Volatility indices stream over one connection. Each index is scored on its last closed candle, and the list is ranked by confirmed signals first, then by how close price is to a well scored zone. Alerts fire for any index, not only the one on the chart. Backtest and stats cover the index on the chart.

    The audit. Every signal scoring 40 or more is logged and resolved on later candles. Each target is counted on its own: a trade that touches 2R and then stops out counts as a win at 1R and 2R, a loss at 3R. Backtest rows come from the loaded history. Live rows are signals that fired while this page was open and are saved in this browser.

    Deriv synthetic indices are random number generated. Read the table above before you trust any score band, and ignore rows with fewer than 30 trades.
